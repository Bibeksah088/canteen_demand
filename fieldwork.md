# Fieldwork Documentation: College Canteen Visit

**Location:** Main Campus Student Canteen (Ground Floor, North Block)  
**Date & Time of Visit:** November 11, 2026 | 11:30 AM – 1:45 PM  
**Person Interviewed:** Manoj Kumar (Head Cook & Canteen In-charge)  

---

### 1. The Interview

#### Person Details
* **Name:** Manoj Kumar  
* **Role:** Head Cook & Daily Operations In-charge (managing food prep and afternoon sales for 6 years)  

#### 3 Key Quotes / Statements from Manoj:
1. *"The hardest part is 1:00 PM. If we run out of Thali or Samosas then, students get annoyed and walk to the outside tapri. But if we make an extra degchi and it rains or students have sudden lab internals, it sits there and goes sour by evening."*
2. *"Friday afternoon is completely unpredictable. Morning batches sell out fast, but after 2:30 PM the campus empties out because hostelers travel home and day scholars skip the last lecture."*
3. *"We don't weigh ingredients or count portions on paper; I just tell the boys to fry 'three trays' or boil 'two big pots' of dal based on how crowded the morning tea stall felt."*

#### 1 Thing That Surprised Me
* **The Surprise:** I expected him to worry primarily about money lost from thrown-away food. Instead, his biggest anxiety was the *social embarrassment and confrontation* with regular final-year students when food runs out before 1:30 PM. To protect against angry students complaining at the counter, he intentionally overcooks everyday staples—even though he knows 10–20 portions will likely be dumped into the waste drum at 4 PM.

### 3. Data Collection Methodology
* **How data was collected:** Recorded over real service days across peak lunch items (`Chai`, `Samosa`, `Veg Thali`, `Fried Rice`).
* **Source:** Crossed-checked morning prep batch counts from kitchen staff against token counter receipts issued between 11:30 AM and 3:30 PM, then verified remaining unsold portions in trays at closing.
* **Integrity Note:** Quantities reflect raw, unrounded counts as recorded directly from the counter registers and kitchen pots.
