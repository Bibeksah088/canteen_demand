# Research: College Canteen Daily Demand Estimator

**Target Person:** Manoj Kumar (Head Cook & Canteen Manager)  
**Location:** Main Campus Student Canteen (Ground Floor, North Block)  
**Date:** November 11, 2026  

---

### 1. The Problem (in 5 lines)
1. The college canteen cooks large batches of daily lunch staples based on static kitchen intuition rather than quantifiable past demand.
2. During mid-week rush hours, popular items sell out before 1:15 PM, causing lost revenue and turning away frustrated students.
3. On low-footfall days (Fridays, post-exam periods, rainy weather), 15% to 25% of cooked food remains unsold as kitchen waste.
4. Perishable cooked dishes cannot be safely stored or carried over overnight due to strict freshness and food safety concerns.
5. The manager needs an ultra-simple tool to recommend daily preparation quantities from recent sales and leftover numbers.

---

### 2. Existing Solutions & Why They Do Not Fit

#### Solution 1: Enterprise Restaurant POS & Inventory ERP (e.g., Petpooja / Posist / Toast)
* **Why it does not fit:** These systems demand expensive recurring software licenses, specialized POS touchscreen hardware, and meticulous recipe-level ingredient logging (Bill of Materials). A busy college canteen counter operated by 2–3 rushed staff cannot halt peak lunch lines to log granular recipe components.

#### Solution 2: Spreadsheet / Excel Tracking Templates
* **Why it does not fit:** Managing spreadsheets on a laptop inside a humid, greasy kitchen environment is impractical. Generic spreadsheets lack automated safety buffer adjustments for perishable items and require manual formula maintenance that non-technical staff frequently break or abandon.

---

### 3. Sources Read & Notes

#### Source 1: UNEP Food Waste Index Report (Food Services & Institutional Dining Chapter)
* **Note 1:** Highlights that educational and institutional catering facilities produce significant avoidable food waste primarily due to uncalibrated batch overproduction.
* **Note 2:** Identifies that tracking three daily metrics—quantity prepared, quantity sold, and remaining leftovers—provides sufficient variance to cut kitchen prep error by over 30%.
* **Note 3:** Stresses that small food operators need frictionless feedback loops rather than complicated supply-chain software to maintain compliance.

#### Source 2: Leanpath & ReFED Practical Dining Case Study ("Production Planning in Campus Food Services")
* **Note 1:** Found kitchen cooks routinely overproduce by 20% as an emotional hedge against the embarrassment of running out of food during peak rush.
* **Note 2:** Demonstrated that providing an empirical "recommended prep quantity" with a small dynamic buffer stabilizes kitchen output across varied days of the week.
* **Note 3:** Concluded that any tool designed for fast-paced kitchens must require less than one minute of daily data entry to ensure continuous real-world use.
